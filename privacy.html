1. Who we are

Opsis AI ("Opsis", "we", "us") provides a Windows desktop application and website that let you ask questions about what is on your screen. This policy explains what personal data we handle when you use them, and it applies to the desktop app, this website, and our API.

For the purposes of the UK/EU GDPR, Opsis AI is the data controller for the information described below.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • We do not run analytics or advertising trackers, and we set no advertising cookies.
  • We do not log keystrokes, monitor your screen in the background, or record audio or video.
  • We do not store the text of your questions or the AI's answers on our servers. Your recent answers history lives only in local storage on your own machine and is cleared when you sign out.

3. How screen captures are handled

This is the part most people want to understand, so here is the full path a capture takes:

  1. You press Analyze, use the keyboard shortcut, or drag a region. Nothing is captured until you do.
  2. The app captures the image locally and compresses it.
  3. The image is sent over TLS to our API, which forwards it to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate an answer.
  4. The answer comes back to your app. The image is held in memory only for the duration of the request and is never written to a database or to disk on our servers.

We record technical facts about the request — its size, its cost, how long it took — because we need them to bill credits and to detect abuse. We do not record the contents.

Please be aware of what is on your screen. A capture includes everything visible in the captured area. If a window contains passwords, medical records, financial details, someone else's personal information, or material covered by a confidentiality or professional obligation, that content will be transmitted to our AI provider. Close or cover anything you do not want to send, or use region select to capture only the part you need.

Caching

To avoid charging you twice for the same question, our servers briefly hold a recent answer in memory, keyed to your account, for up to five minutes. It is not written to disk and it is discarded when the time expires or the server restarts.

Model training

Your screen content is not used to train our models or our provider's models. Anthropic's commercial terms for API traffic state that customer inputs and outputs are not used to train its models. Anthropic may retain API content for a limited period for trust and safety purposes; see Anthropic's privacy policy.

4. Why we process it, and our legal basis

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to create your account, process your captures into answers, track credits, and deliver the service you paid for.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, understand aggregate cost and performance, and fix faults. We have considered your interests and limited what we collect accordingly.
  • Legal obligation — to keep the tax and accounting records the law requires.

We do not rely on consent for any of the above, and we do not carry out profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

7. Your rights and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a portable copy, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. If you are in California, you additionally have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Doing it yourself, immediately

Two of these are built into the product, so you do not have to ask us or wait:

  • Export — the Privacy section of your account page downloads everything we hold about you as a JSON file.
  • Delete — the same page permanently deletes your account, credits, ledger and request metadata, and cancels any active subscription. This cannot be undone.

For anything else, or to make a request on behalf of someone else, contact us using the details in section 12. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder before we act on it, which normally means sending the request from the email address on the account.

If you are in the UK or EU and you believe we have handled your data improperly, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

The measures we take include:

  • All traffic between the app, the website and our API is encrypted with TLS.
  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; nobody at Opsis can read them.
  • Sessions use short-lived, cryptographically signed tokens.
  • Database access is restricted by row-level security, so one account cannot read another's records.
  • Screen captures are never written to persistent storage, which means there is no archive of them to breach.
  • Rate limiting and abuse controls sit in front of every endpoint.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach occurs that is likely to present a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant regulator within the timeframes the law requires.

9. Children

Opsis is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers are based primarily in the United States, so your data will be processed there. Where data moves out of the UK or the European Economic Area, that transfer is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard in our agreements with each provider.
